Robust Real-vs-AI Image Detection & Generator Attribution

Self-supervised representation learning, latent-space analysis & explainable AI

Can we tell a real photo from an AI-generated one, and figure out which generator made it? I'm studying representations that hold up across unseen generators and everyday image edits.

  • Testing robustness to compression, resizing and post-processing.
